Randomness & Probability EnginesThese functions generate entropy or resolve uncertainty.Dice Rolling (rollDice)Parameters: count (number of dice), sides (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100), customFaces (array of strings/icons instead of numbers), dropLowest/keepHighest (for advantage/disadvantage), rerollConditions (e.g., exploding dice on max roll).Bag / Pool Building & Drawing (drawFromPool)Description: Drawing tokens/cubes from an opaque bag without replacement until a reshuffle trigger occurs.Parameters: poolID, drawCount, replaceAfterDraw (boolean).Spinner / Wheel (spinWheel)Parameters: segments (array of weighted outcomes).Coin Flip / Binary Check (flipCoin)Parameters: probability (default 0.5).2. Card & Deck ManipulationEssential for any card-driven game, deck-builder, or hand-management system.Deck Lifecycle (shuffle, draw, discard, mill, exile/trash)Description: Moving card entities between distinct zones (Draw Pile, Hand, Discard Pile, Play Area, Removed from Game).Parameters: sourceZone, targetZone, count, position (top, bottom, random).Deck Building (acquireCard, trashCard)Description: Permanently adding new cards to a player's personal deck cycle or permanently removing weak starter cards.Tableau Building (playToTableau)Description: Placing cards face-up in front of a player to create permanent passive abilities, resource engines, or point-scoring sets.Drafting (draftPick)Description: Selecting one card from a shared pool (open market) or a rotating hand (closed/passing draft) and passing the remainder to the next player.Parameters: draftType (open market vs. pass-left/right), pickCount.Trick-Taking (evaluateTrick)Description: Players each play one card; the function evaluates a winner based on lead suit, trump suit, and card rank.Parameters: leadSuit, trumpSuit, playedCards[].Multi-Use Cards (resolveCardMode)Description: A single card entity can be executed as an event, tucked as a resource, or discarded for movement points.3. Turn Structure & Action EconomyThese control when players act and how much they can do.Turn Order Management (advanceTurn, setTurnOrder)Modes: Fixed clockwise, variable/bid-based initiative, progressive (last place goes first), or simultaneous action selection.Action Point Allowance (spendActionPoints)Description: Players receive a budget of $X$ points per turn and spend them on a menu of actions with varying costs.Worker Placement (placeWorker, recallWorkers)Description: Moving a limited player token onto a shared board node to trigger an effect, usually blocking other players from using that node until the next round.Parameters: workerType (some workers have unique abilities), nodeID, allowSharedSpace (boolean).Action Selection / Role Selection (selectRole)Description: Players choose a phase/action card from a central pool; all players may get to execute a weaker version of the chosen action ("Follow" mechanic).Rondel (advanceRondel)Description: Moving a marker forward along a circular track of actions; moving 1–3 spaces is free, moving further costs resources.4. Spatial & Board MovementFunctions that govern grids, networks, and physical positioning.Grid / Network Movement (moveEntity)Topologies: Square grid (orthogonal/diagonal), Hex grid, Point-to-Point (graph nodes/edges), or Area-based (irregular regions).Parameters: entityID, path[], movementPoints, terrainCosts.Tile Placement / Map Building (placeTile)Description: Adding new nodes/edges to the board dynamically, checking for edge-matching validation rules (e.g., road-to-road).Parameters: tileID, coordinates, rotation (0, 90, 180, 270 or hex 60° increments).Area Control / Majority (calculateAreaControl)Description: Counting player units/influence within a specific region to determine ownership or distribute tiered rewards (1st, 2nd, 3rd place).Area Enclosure (checkEnclosure)Description: Flood-fill algorithm to detect when a player has completely surrounded a region or group of enemy pieces.Line of Sight / Range (checkLineOfSight, getDistance)Description: Raycasting across grid cells or hexes to determine if obstacles block attacks or visibility.5. Economy & Resource ManagementHandling currencies, conversion engines, and scarcity.Resource Pools (modifyResource)Description: Incrementing/decrementing tracked values (Gold, Wood, Mana, Health) with min/max clamping.Resource Conversion / Engine Building (executeRecipe)Description: Transforming Input A + Input B into Output C (e.g., 2 Wheat + 1 Ore = 1 Victory Point).Market / Dynamic Pricing (buyFromMarket, sellToMarket)Description: Supply-and-demand tracks where buying an item shifts the price marker up for the next buyer, and selling shifts it down.Trading & Negotiation (proposeTrade, executeTrade)Description: Atomic swap of assets between two or more player inventories upon mutual boolean confirmation.6. Conflict, Auctions & Social DeductionAuction / Bidding (runAuction)Variants: Open ascending (English), Single-round sealed bid, Dutch (price drops until someone claims), or Once-around.Combat Resolution (resolveCombat)Variants: Deterministic (Strength A minus Strength B), Dice-pool comparison (compare highest rolls), or Rock-Paper-Scissors simultaneous card reveal.Push Your Luck (pushLuckStep)Description: Repeating a risky draw/roll loop; player can voluntarily bank current gains or continue until a "bust" condition wipes out the turn's progress.Hidden Roles & Alignment (assignSecretRoles, checkWinCondition)Description: Assigning asymmetric victory conditions and team affiliations hidden from other players' state views.Bluffing / Hidden Information (revealHiddenState)Description: Masking specific entity attributes from non-owning players until an inspection or reveal trigger occurs.7. Progression & Victory TriggersTech Trees / Track Advancement (advanceTrack)Description: Moving a marker up a linear track to unlock passive upgrades, increase resource income, or trigger end-game milestones.Set Collection (evaluateSets)Description: Pattern-matching a player's inventory against scoring rules (e.g., $N$ of a kind, sequential runs, or one of each unique type).Legacy / Persistent State (mutateGameState)Description: Writing permanent modifications to cards, boards, or rule flags that persist across separate game sessions.End-Game & Victory Evaluation (checkEndTriggers, calculateFinalScore)Triggers: Empty supply pile, target VP threshold reached, fixed round limit hit, or player elimination.
